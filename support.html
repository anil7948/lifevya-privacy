Common questions

Why does LifeVya need access to my Photos, Contacts, Calendar, etc.?
LifeVya only reads a source after you grant permission, and only to connect your own information — photos, people, events, and documents — into one private, searchable view on your iPhone. You can revoke any permission at any time in iOS Settings; LifeVya keeps working with whatever remains available.

Does my data ever leave my iPhone?
Search, chat, and face grouping stay on your iPhone. There is no LifeVya account, no LifeVya server, and no analytics. Two Apple services can be used: an optional model download, and place-name lookup for photo locations. See the Privacy Policy for the full detail.

My device isn't supported — why?
LifeVya runs its AI models on your iPhone's hardware. That requires an iPhone 15 Pro or newer, on iOS 26 or later. The App Store only offers the app on that hardware. If a device still cannot run it safely, LifeVya explains that instead of running poorly.

How do I delete everything LifeVya has stored?
Go to Settings → Storage & Data → Delete All LifeVya Data. This permanently removes the library LifeVya has built on your iPhone; downloaded AI models and your settings are kept. Deleting the app itself also removes its data.

I found a bug or the app crashed — what should I include in my report?
Please email us with: what you were doing when it happened, your iPhone model and iOS version, and whether it's reproducible. Screenshots help a lot.
